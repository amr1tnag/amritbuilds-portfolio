01. about

      

        

          

            Hey — I'm Amrit. I'm an engineer who cares equally about
            what gets built and how it feels to use. My happy place is
            the layer where systems meet product: making hard things look effortless.
          

          

            I started by breaking things — my first "project" was bricking the family
            computer and having to figure out how to fix it before anyone noticed.
            That reverse-engineering instinct never left. Now I channel it into
            building backends that don't fall over, interfaces that don't make you
            think, and tooling that makes other engineers faster.
          

          

            When I'm not shipping, I'm reading systems papers, contributing to open
            source, or over-optimizing something that absolutely did not need
            optimizing. No regrets.
